Hi, I'm Rebecca! 👋

I'm a conservation geneticist fascinated by the stories hidden in DNA. From cold-water mountain streams to salty seas and ocean dreams, I use genomic data to understand population structure, investigate how populations adapt to changing environments, and inform conservation and management.

Research

My research focuses on population genomics, conservation genetics, and evolutionary biology. I’m especially interested in using genomic data to understand population structure, how environmental factors influence organismal fitness, and the evolutionary forces shaping genetic variation

My work has included freshwater fish systems such as Brook Trout, and I’m now expanding into marine genomics through postdoctoral research on sea scallops.
